- Implements retryable Position blocked and staging-unsafe handling with persistent Retry/Dismiss actions.

These are implemented safeguards with automated regression coverage. Live Race and Preview behavior remains pending owner validation.

## Known limitations

- Gameplay, visuals, Preview renderer behavior, controller/UINav, UI scale/safe zones, locale rendering, third-party vehicles and performance require the exact downloaded published ZIP in BeamNG.drive 0.39.4.
- Pega-Pega/Tag remains a non-user-facing foundation; Infection and other party modes are future work.
- BeamMP discovery/networking is not included or claimed supported.
- Broad `main.lua` and `apiAdapter.lua` decomposition remains v0.8.0 debt.

The deterministic ZIP, `.zip.sha256` and manifest are the only supported release assets. GitHub source archives are not mod packages.
