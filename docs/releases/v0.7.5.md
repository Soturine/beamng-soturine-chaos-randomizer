# Soturine's Chaos Randomizer v0.7.5

Post-v0.7.4 live-findings **experimental prerelease** targeting BeamNG 0.39.4.

## Fixed and changed

- Separates applied outcomes from verification confidence with explicit
  completed, skipped, warning, partial-applied, failure, rollback and cancel
  codes. Unknown fluid telemetry is a warning, not a false partial result.
- Uses phase-aware semantic liveness so duplicate callbacks cannot renew a
  watchdog, valid engine waits are distinct from no progress, and hard
  deadlines remain bounded.
- Batches Full Random part decisions, enforces four-reload/one-repair bounds,
  records tuning reloads and semantic activity, and reports genuinely bounded
  incomplete mutations as `PARTIAL_APPLIED`.
- Makes Race Preview observability explicit: data-ready, unavailable renderer,
  render error, visible, stale and disabled are distinct; visibility requires
  at least one successfully rendered marker in a frame.
- Restores player focus after background spawn/switch, applies owned staging at
  first candidate spawn, protects accepted slots, keeps Mods Showcase strict on
  runtime/ownership but permissive on stable unusual drivability, and contains
  persistence/scheduler failure per lineup and slot.
- Renames the normal Race destination to Events and uses Setup, Formation,
  Behavior and Start with simple AI presets and advanced disclosure. Layout is
  intrinsic inside measured AppHost bounds and avoids viewport-based popups.
- Aligns backend/UI AI modes and adds Follow, Convoy, Chase, Flee, Traffic,
  Roam and deterministic mixed-role Swarm presets.
- Adds Tag/contact state-machine foundations and authority-aware vehicle
  identity. Tag is not user-facing and BeamMP is not claimed supported.
- Applies the localization termbase and keeps raw phase/outcome codes out of
  normal translated presentation.
