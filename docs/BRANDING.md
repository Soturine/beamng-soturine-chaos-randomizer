# Brand assets

Soturine's Chaos Randomizer uses a project-owned generated raster fox as its primary mark. The committed source-quality runtime variant is `fox-1024.png`; `fox-256.png` and `fox-64.png` serve normal and compact UI densities, and `app.png` is the transparent BeamNG app-selector crop.

All runtime variants are local static PNGs. They are distributed under the repository's Apache-2.0 licensing terms, contain no third-party logo or watermark, and require no remote fetch or generation at runtime. Keep the fox aspect ratio, transparent padding and orange/white/charcoal palette intact. Do not recreate it as a low-detail geometric SVG.

Generation and alpha-processing evidence for the current files is recorded in the versioned [v0.7.6 asset report](testing/v0.7.6/FOX_ASSET.md).
