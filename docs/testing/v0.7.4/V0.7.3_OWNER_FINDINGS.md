# v0.7.3 owner findings carried into v0.7.4

Status: **Executed live against the published v0.7.3 ZIP; mixed result**.

These observations were supplied by the owner after the v0.7.3 prerelease. They are live-derived input for v0.7.4, not a retroactive pass of the historical v0.7.3 report. Exact event counts were not supplied and are not invented here.

## Confirmed improvements

- The native Vue Runtime UI mounted successfully; the earlier `.some` placement failure did not recur.
- `BngSmartSelect` accepted selection input.
- AppHost compact/expanded outer geometry changed as expected.
- Random Car completed replacement faster than the older pipeline.
- Scramble mutated the existing vehicle.
- Full Random was materially more efficient than v0.7.2 and did not reproduce the earlier uncontrolled vehicle proliferation.
- Generated results were visibly chaotic.
- A Race configured for four total vehicles correctly planned three opponents plus the player.
- Seeds were distinct and operation/generation diagnostics were visible.

## Confirmed remaining failures

- Random Car could complete a valid replacement and then be reported as a cardinality failure.
- Scramble could intermittently report false cardinality or Safety failure after mutating the same vehicle.
- Transient post-reload reads could be promoted to invalid before the vehicle tree converged.
- Runtime integrity, drivability, chaos-policy acceptance, and engine-fluid telemetry were not separated sufficiently.
- Full Random still showed avoidable reload/readback churn.
- Raw phase/status codes and technical English notifications remained visible in normal UI paths.
- Recovery could temporarily remove or hide the controlled vehicle and produce confusing focus/lifecycle feedback.
- Responsive layout followed the game viewport rather than the actual AppHost width; narrow Garage cards broke labels vertically.
- Lock management remained too prominent and its category classifier could let generic ancestry override a specific leaf.
- Race summary/policy copy remained noisy, an English empty-state string leaked, dropdown placement could escape the app, and compact content remained too dense.
- The persistent idle `Ready` footer was unnecessary.

## Race execution

The owner ran a four-vehicle Race (player plus three planned opponents) with the Mods Showcase policy. All three opponent slots failed. Candidate vehicles were removed, the lineup ended with `lineup_storage_failed`, and the camera followed background candidates during generation. This confirms:

- player focus isolation failed;
- accepted-slot retention and slot-local failure containment were not established live;
- partial/invalid competitor DNA reached the lineup storage path;
- physical accepted counts did not match the intended lineup.

## Evidence boundary

These notes do not establish renderer, UI-scale, safe-zone, controller, performance, or gameplay approval for v0.7.4. Every v0.7.4 live case remains pending until it is executed against the exact published v0.7.4 ZIP.
