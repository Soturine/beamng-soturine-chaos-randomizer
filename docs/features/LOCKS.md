# Locks

Locks can preserve the vehicle, configuration, evidence categories, normalized slot paths, current parts, tuning variables/categories, and paint fields. Reroll Unlocked restores and verifies its parent before applying independent creative substreams.

Unavailable or ambiguous targets are reported rather than remapped by a similar name. Lock persistence is opt-in.

See the detailed [Locks guide](../LOCKS.md) and [Mutations](../MUTATIONS.md).
